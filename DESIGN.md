# Visual system
TypeSafe.AI-inspired scientific desktop. Black window title bars, restrained squared borders, Space Grotesk type, Plex Mono for measurements, pink population pixels on pale blue water. Adapt the reference's retro research instruments and pink/blue contrast, without reproducing its marketing content or decorative cloud scene.

Mode: Operate. First viewport: immediate experiment controls, a real geographic population field, timeline and observations. Signature interaction: scrubbing time repaints the population field and synchronizes counts and the chart cursor.

Tokens: ink #20211f; canvas #f2f3ed; panel #fafbf6; border #acafa5; accent #f44dc8; water #d9edf2; land #e8e9df. Body Space, measurement Plex; desktop gaps 18px; main margin 32px; mobile margin 14px. Windows have square corners, a single 1px border and no shadow. Focus uses #9b126c with 3px outline.

Mobile: map and playback first, settings second, observations third; no hidden settings or alternate data. Reduced motion disables smooth scrolling; playback is opt-in. Labels and native form semantics remain available without the map.
