# Dataset attribution

Binford (2001), Constructing Frames of Reference. Maintained dataset: Ben Marwick, Amber Johnson, Doug White and E. Anthon Eff, R package binford0.1.0. Pinned repository revision and input hashes are in acquisition.json. The package declares GPL-3; COPYING accompanies the redistributed data and derived observation table. Original ethnographic references and dates are retained in calibration-observations.csv. This new fit is not endorsed by the source authors.

Beyer, Krapp & Manica (2020), DOI10.1038/s41597-020-0552-1, with pastclim v1.1.0 packaging at Zenodo7062281. Climate data CC BY4.0. The compact baseline extraction preserves original values; the script derives a modern density-reference surface with explicit unknown and out-of-domain cells.
